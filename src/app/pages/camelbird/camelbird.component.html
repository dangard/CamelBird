Camelbird The Story

It started back in high school...

My friend Mike was super into Origami and was always creating little origami creatures and shapes. One day he started making a crane and decided to give it legs for fun. I saw this legged crane and decided to launch into its backstory. So I started the tale of the great North American flightless Camelbird, that once ranged across the great plains of North America. Unfortunately due to habitat destruction and overhunting, for their insulating feathers, they are practically extinct. Small flocks exist in remote corners of the great plains. Naturally they congregate in small family groups, but there have been mutterings of larger gatherings in the remote corners of the plains. Some folktales forewarn that they are gathering strength and will one day rise up and reclaim the plains for their own.

So when it came down to looking for domains for my portfolio site I thought it would be fun to base it on a funny story from growing up.
