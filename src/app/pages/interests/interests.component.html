Interests

Learning

One very pertinent video they have is this one on Vaccines. Some other videos I have really enjoyed include The Skyhook. Alien Scale (The Kardashev Scale). Hobbies

For relaxation I really enjoy playing video games. World of Warcraft, Enshrouded, Dragon Age: The Veilguard, and Wayfinder are a few of my current favorites. Will always have a special place in my heart for the Zelda Games.

Since I love video games, I've been trying my hand at creating addons and game mods. I've created some XML config override mods for Mass Effect and I'm working on some Custom Content items for Sims 4.

I'm an avid reader mostly of sci-fi/fantasy. Some of my favorite authors include Isaac Asimov, Terry Pratchett, Terry Brooks, Stephen King, Dean Koontz, Jim Butcher, and Robert Jordan. I think of programming as a bit magical. I work in arcane languages that I can use to make things happen all the time.
